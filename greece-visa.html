Greece Farm Work Visa Guidance

Complete Legal Framework, Requirements & Application Roadmap

Program Overview

The Greece Farm Work Visa offers legal agricultural employment opportunities for international workers in Greece. Under the expert legal consultancy of Naeem Shehzad (15+ years experience), Liberty Global Services provides comprehensive guidance to ensure full compliance with Greek immigration laws and European labor guidelines.

Key Documentation & Requirements

  • Valid International Passport: Must have at least 12–18 months validity remaining.
  • Clean Criminal Record: Official Police Clearance Certificate duly attested.
  • Medical Fitness Certificate: Issued by authorized medical institutions proving fitness for agricultural labor.
  • Official Greek Work Permit / Approval: Authorization issued by local Greek authorities or employers.
  • Attestation & Legal Verification: Proper document verification before embassy submission.

Step-by-Step Application Process

Step 1: Initial Profile Evaluation

We review your background and documentation to confirm full eligibility before commencing any formal legal filings.

Step 2: Document Compilation & Attestation

Gathering all legal papers, medical records, and police clearances, ensuring exact embassy specifications are met.

Step 3: Work Permit Processing

Coordinating the filing and legal verification of official employment permissions from Greek relevant authorities.

Step 4: Embassy Interview Coaching

One-on-one preparation sessions with Naeem Shehzad to ensure confidence and accuracy during official embassy appointments.
